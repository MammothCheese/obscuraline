#  Every word that shows up in the game. Edit freely, then build (tools/content.py checks it).
#
#  [chapter id]   one per chapter in puzzles.txt (same id):
#      numeral    = I                      shown before the title in the header (empty for the prologue)
#      title      = THE COURIER'S HEART    the header, the title screen's chapter list
#      help_title = REGULATE THE BEAT      the instruction card's heading
#      help       = ...                    the card's text: one line here, wrapped on screen, 200 characters at most
#      footer     = K1 RATE  K2 BEAT       the control legend: TWO spaces separate items (each gets its own colour),
#                                          30 characters at most
#      word       = TRUE                   engraved on the plaque when it is solved, 10 characters at most
#      done       = P-9 NOMINAL            (optional) a line some mechanisms show in their finale
#      in:  art | voice | text             a story page before the puzzle
#      out: art | voice | text             a story page after it
#
#      art:   platform train cab signalbox workshop map timetable pocketwatch harbour archive tunnel
#             observatory depot ghost door heart sakura keeper darktrain office seatram festtram   (tools/style/line_art.py; a new one: add it there)
#      voice: robot  (cyan capitals, a lower tick: the line's machines speaking)   or  -  (narration, cream)
#      text:  about 150 characters at most (five lines on screen)
#
#  [ui]       short texts the mechanisms and menus show (key = text). Keep them about as long as they are:
#             most sit in fixed places on a 240 pixel screen (8 pixels a character).
#  [credits]  the closing scroll: gold: (Deco capitals), text:, neon: (cyan), gap:
#
# =====================================================================================================================
